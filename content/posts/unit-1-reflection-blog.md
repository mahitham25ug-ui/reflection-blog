The _Unit 1_ felt quite easy compared to _Unit 2_. My understanding about data and information changed as we went through _DIKW model_. Overall, Unit 1 was interesting as we got a clear idea about data, information, knowlegde and wisdom and also learned new skills on developing website and publishing our own blog.
