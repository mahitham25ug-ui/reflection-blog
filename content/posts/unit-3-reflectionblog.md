## UNIT 3 - REFLECTION

Unit 3 was a bit different compared to Unit 1 and 2. It helped me understand about failures. The readings and case studies assigned for us made me understand more about how mistakes and failures happen. It helped me understand that failures are not random events but are results of so many small mistakes. 

I also learned that small mistakes should be taken into concern so that big failures could be avoided. The readings contained so many examples which explained how these small mistakes are resulting into major failures. 

The case study that was given for our group dealt with a real life failure and through that case study, we studied how life of people get affected through these complex problems and how people often tend to blame a single person for a major failure without even checking whether it is a system error.

In conclusion, the unit helped me learn about different aspects of failures through the readings and real life examples in a detailed way and the interesting part was, as the case study had to be explored by ourselves, it made us look through the case in a more detailed way. And overall the unit felt quite interesing for me.
