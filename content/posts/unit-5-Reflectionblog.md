# UNIT 5 REFLECTION

I would like to write my reflection blog of Unit 5 about the presentation by Group no. 4, which was about the experiences of women in the field of computer science. Among all nine presentations, this was the one that I found interesting. They discussed the history where there were many contributions of women in various fields of computer science, and also explained how these are dominated now by men. Their way of presenting the topic was more attention-grabbing. They also discussed the challenges faced by women in computing-related fields. They explained how society and culture have an influence on women emerging in computing.

I felt the topic to be more relevant in today's context, as most of the IT field is dominated by men, and the condition where women are not given equal exposure to technology as given to men. The society usually considers that areas such as computing are for men, and women are not given proper encouragement to work with tech. The group also explained how these challenges affect women and their confidence. And this also leads women to believe that they are not capable enough to enter the field of technology. And I believe that in the present context, where there are still disparities that women face when they decide to take up technological paths as their career, discussing such topics is important.

I felt the presentation would be better if they went into the topic more deeply. But overall, I felt that the group presented the topic with clarity and was also engaging. And I do believe and agree with the ideas in their presentations that women are facing challenges and disparities in the IT field, and I also believe that it is high time that we should make people aware that women are also capable of doing tech-related jobs, and also build confidence within the women who have the passion to pursue Computer science-related jobs but are not taking the steps towards it.
