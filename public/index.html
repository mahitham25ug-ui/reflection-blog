UNIT 3 - REFLECTION

Unit 3 was a bit different compared to Unit 1 and 2. It helped me understand about failures. The readings and case studies assigned for us made me understand more about how mistakes and failures happen. It helped me understand that failures are not random events but are results of so many small mistakes.

I also learned that small mistakes should be taken into concern so that big failures could be avoided. The readings contained so many examples which explained how these small mistakes are resulting into major failures.

UNIT 2 - REFLECTION

Unit 2 was difficult as well as an interesting unit. The most interesting session of the unit was learning about images. This session helped us know more on how an image is represented in computers and how to calculate the memory required to store an image as well as videos. The idea that the range of colours having different intensity values which is represented with numbers was a new learning for me from the unit.
