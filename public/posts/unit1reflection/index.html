UNIT 1 - REFLECTION

The Unit 1 felt quite easy compared to Unit 2. My understanding about data and information changed as we went through DIKW model. Earlier I thought data and information shares somewhat the same idea, but as we discussed in DIKW model, the raw data is getting processed to information. Further, the activities conducted in class such as the census of bangalore helped me study about the interpretation of data in real life.

And the next session I found interesting was working with markdown language and wiki editing. The curiosity behind, how the wikipedia and other websites organizes and edits knowledge and then share it worldwide, got satisfied. The exciting part was that we learned to create our own blog and publish it online. Setting up of blog had been a complicated and difficult task, but still we managed to do it with the help of our instructor.

Overall, Unit 1 was interesting as we got a clear idea about data, information, knowlegde and wisdom and also learned new skills on developing website and publishing our own blog.
