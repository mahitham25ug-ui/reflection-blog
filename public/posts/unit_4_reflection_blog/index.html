UNIT 4 REFLECTION

Working with the two projects felt really interesting as it was different from what we usually do in our lab sessions. The most interesting part was us exploring the tools on our own without any provided guidelines. At first, I thought that working alone would be hard, but the process was really interesting as we could go through most of the features possible of the tool. And I think that working with proper guidelines would not give us as much fun as we had because if there are guidelines, we would only explore the mentioned features of the tool rather than exploring as a whole as we did.

Out of the two projects, working with the Metabase tool was the one which i found more interesting. Through the Metabase project, I was able to learn to communicate and visualize data properly with more clarity and better insight. The project, even though it was difficult at first, through constant exploration, became easy to work with. And also proper planning was the crucial factor that helped me execute the project in a better way. The Chatbot project also needed proper planning because we had to decide which questions the user would ask after asking one question, what the reader expects us to answer, etc. But compared to the Metabase project, the Chatbot project was a bit complex. Connecting the chats one with the other was a bit confusing.

Overall, the two projects were interesting and informative in a way that both developed and improved our skills to communicate and visualize data properly with the reader through hands-on exploration and working with the tools. Also, the challenges we faced while working on the projects helped me understand the possible areas where mistakes would happen while dealing with a huge set of data. I believe that the learnings from these two projects would truly help me in my upcoming academic journey.
