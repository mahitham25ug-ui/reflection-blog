REFLECTION BLOG

Hello Everyone,

I am Mahitha, currently doing my undergraduate degree from Azim Premji University. I am doing my major in BSc Information Science. I am writing this blog to reflect on my classroom discussions and also share some of my learning experiences.

I am a person who loves to learn new things, but at sometimes, i feel nervous when it comes to learning technical subjects like information science. But my strong will for not giving up, helps me in understanding the subject step by step.

The lab sessions were very interesting as we learned many things like about markdown language, wiki file, hugo, GitHub and many more. I actually had no idea about how a website is created and published online. But through the lab session I came to know more about it and how it works.

At first, I found some difficulty in creating the GitHub account and adding the SSH key as it was a bit confusing. But after following the steps slowly as per mentioned and also with help of the peer group, I was able to finish the work. And working with markdown language and Zettlr was also new and I thought it would be a difficult task, but it went smoothly.

Writing in Markdown language was simple yet different experience for me from the normal word document. Through the lab sessions, I learned how to create a new site, create a post and publish it. As the learning was step by step, as we learned Markdown basics first and then practiced wiki and finally created a blog website, so this kind of learning made it easier.

through this blog, I hope i would be able to improve my tech skills and confidence. This reflection writing helped me recall what all things we learned in class.
